## What this tells us about the voice (observations, not rules yet)

1. **The brand defines itself by what it refuses to do**: flashy ads, fear-based marketing, influencer route, "chemical-free"/"100% natural" framing, gimmicks. The negative space is the brand.
2. **Education is the mode.** "Explanatory posts", "ingredient and product education", "people want to understand what they're using and why." An ad that only asserts a result, without explaining what the ingredient is or does, is off-voice even if the claim is true.
3. **Concentration is identity.** The % is in the product name itself. Dropping it is like dropping the brand name.
4. **Authority = dermatologists and data**, not celebrities or influencers.
5. **Tension on the record:** the homepage runs "Upto 33% OFF + Freebies" and "🎁" banners alongside the transparency pillars. Promotional voice exists at the brand.
